Trace It: Procedures and Nested Loops

Two(2) procedures. Both take arguments, and both have a loop inside a loop where the inner loop depends on those arguments. Predict first, then step through and see where you were off.

Why this feels harder than Snap!

Two(2) things are different here, and they are the two(2) things that trip people up.

1. Nothing labels the slots. In Snap! a block says seats (rows:) (firstRow:) — the slot tells you what goes in it. In seats(5, 12) the position is the only thing telling you which is which. First argument goes to the first parameter. Always.

2. The inner loop runs a different number of times on every pass. In Snap! you usually see repeat (10) and 10 is just 10. Here the inner loop says REPEAT inRow TIMES, and the outer loop changes inRow every time around. So "how many times does the inner loop run?" has a different answer each pass.

Note: finish a step before the next one opens. If you are stuck, you can open it anyway — but guess first. Guessing and being wrong is how this one sticks.
