Two-Player Mastermind

Red means right color, right spot. White means right color, wrong spot.

Player 1 makes a secret code of 4 colors. Player 2 has 10 guesses to crack it. After every guess, Player 1 gives the clues. That clue step is the part your program will do.

How Player 1 gives the clues

Compare the guess to the secret code and say two numbers:

Right color, right spot Right color, wrong spot

Count the right-spot pegs first. A peg that already counted can't count again. Example:

Blue is in the right spot: 1 red. Red and Yellow are in the code but in the wrong spots: 2 white. Green isn't in the code at all.
