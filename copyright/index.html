Copyright & Fair Use

Unit 2, Lab 5. Work through these in order with your partner. Everything you need is on this page.

Before you start

Open your pair working doc — one per pair — and keep it beside these pages. You will be asked to write in it as you go, not at the end.

Part 1 is the exception: do it on your own device, by yourself. It is a survey, and it only works if nobody is looking over your shoulder.

The six parts

Would You? Should You? Two short sets of questions about what you actually do and what you think should be allowed. Answer honestly — it is anonymous. on your own · ~15 min Fair Use Court Nine real cases, from 2 Live Crew to the AI lawsuits. Agree on a verdict with your partner before you reveal what the court said. in pairs · ~25 min Build a License Where Creative Commons came from, and why it was built after losing in court. Then make your own license and see exactly what you gave away. in pairs · ~17 min Copyright Clock Copyright is supposed to expire. Change the rule from 1790 to today and find out when Mickey, Gatsby and your own work go free. in pairs · ~13 min Locked You paid for it, so use it. Five locks say no, and the law says you may not pick them — even though every one of those uses was legal. in pairs · ~18 min Cite Your Project A made-up Snap! project with seven borrowed pieces. Work out which ones need crediting, which one you cannot use at all, and how to write each credit. in pairs · ~15 min

What you turn in

Pair working doc One per pair. Each part tells you what to write in it. Follow-up, on your own · 10 points Individual. Do this after the five parts.
