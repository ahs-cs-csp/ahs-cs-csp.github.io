The four factors

Courts weigh all four. No single one decides a case by itself, and there is no percentage or number of seconds that makes copying automatically legal.

  1. Purpose and character of the use
    Is it transformative — does it add new meaning, or just repackage the original? Is it commercial? Teaching, criticism, news and parody help here.
  2. Nature of the copyrighted work
    Copying from factual work is easier to defend than copying from something highly creative, like a song or a novel.
  3. Amount and substantiality
    How much did you take — and was it the heart of the work? A few seconds can be too much if it is the part everyone recognizes.
  4. Effect on the market
    Does your use replace sales of the original, or cut off a market the owner would normally license?
